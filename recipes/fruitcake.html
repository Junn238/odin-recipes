FruitCake

Description

This cake, prepared in Hateno according to a well-defined recipe, consists of a batter mixed with baking powder, filled with candied fruits and berries, topped with a variety of fruit slices, and baked in a special pan that every village have around its community kitchen.

A well-made cake requires meticulous attention to the proportions of sugar and flour so that the fruit is evenly distributed. The baking process must also be carried out correctly: with a fairly high flame at first, then gradually reduced.

Ingredients

  • One among a variety of Apples and Wildberries.
  • One among a variety of tropical fruits like: Hearty Durian, Coconut, Voltfruit or Mighty Bananas.
  • Wheat, preferably from Tabantha.
  • Cane Sugar.

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients and start the fire.
  2. Prepare the cake: Mix together the cake mix (the wheat and sugarcane, previously processed at your local mill, along with a little bit of water) and pour it into three greased and rounded pans.
  3. Bake in the previously started fire until the cake pieces look baked.
  4. Spread frosting onto the cooled cake layers. Stack layers; frost top and sides and put fruit slices between the cake layers.
  5. Finally, add more pieces of fruit on the top of the cake and enjoy.
